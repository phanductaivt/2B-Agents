# PO BACCM Framing Guide

Use BACCM to frame the work before BA expands it into functional detail.

## Elements To Cover

- `Change`
  - what business change is being pursued
- `Need`
  - what problem or opportunity creates the need
- `Solution`
  - what product direction is intended at a high level
- `Stakeholder`
  - who is affected, who benefits, who decides
- `Value`
  - what value the business expects
- `Context`
  - what constraints, market conditions, or operating realities shape the work

## Working Rule

- if BACCM framing is weak, fix the BRD before BA expands the package
- keep this framing product-facing, not technical
