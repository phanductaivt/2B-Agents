# Checklist - BRD

- [ ] Business objective is stated.
- [ ] Business problem and stakeholder impact are stated.
- [ ] BACCM framing is visible.
- [ ] In-scope and out-of-scope items are listed.
- [ ] First release slice is visible.
- [ ] Business, stakeholder, solution, and transition requirements are classified.
- [ ] Observed market signals are separated from inference and recommendation when market research matters.
- [ ] Business rules are listed.
- [ ] Policy or operational constraints are listed when relevant.
- [ ] Stakeholders are identified.
- [ ] Success measures are stated or explicitly marked as still missing.
- [ ] Linked REQ ID and BRD ID are shown.
