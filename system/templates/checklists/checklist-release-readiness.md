# Checklist - Release Readiness

- [ ] Blockers are separated from risks.
- [ ] Major business-impacting risks are visible.
- [ ] Acceptable known gaps are listed explicitly.
- [ ] Readiness recommendation is explicit.
- [ ] Authorization, pricing, payment, dependency, and customer-impact concerns are called out when relevant.
